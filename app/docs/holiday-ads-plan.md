# Holiday video ads: "your new ad is ready" for every major holiday

Owner idea, 2026-11-06. Our best email by far is "your video ad for {site} is ready":
- 60 to 76% open
- most sales come within 20 minutes of it

So we send that moment to every lead again, before each major holiday, with a free holiday version of their video ad. Each one costs us about $1, and each one is a new offer to someone who already knows us.

## How it works
1. **About 10 days before each holiday,** the pipeline makes a holiday version of every lead's video ad (all free video leads and past buyers whose site has a real domain).
2. **The video never invents a discount.** It says, for example, "Black Friday special at {business}, call or visit us". The free change is where the owner adds their real offer, and that is what gets them to reply.
3. **The email:**
   > Subject: {name}, your {holiday} video ad is ready
   >
   > Hi {name},
   >
   > I made a {holiday} video ad for {business}, free. It's ready to post on Facebook, Instagram and your website.
   >
   > If you have a special offer, tell me and we'll put it in the video for free.
   >
   > Watch it here: [link]
   >
   > Szilard
4. **What we sell with it:**
   - $49 removes the logo, the same as today
   - a holiday pack bundles the next 2 or 3 holidays clean, for example "Black Friday + Christmas + New Year, all clean, $97"
