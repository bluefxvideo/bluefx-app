## 2. The Structure and Why

1. **Format: founder demonstration.** A live result is proof that's hard to fake.
2. **On camera: Szilard, selfie.** His list bought at 39% versus 10% for cold leads, so trust in him sells. The AI presenter appears only in the result.
3. **Length: about 70 seconds.** Enough to show the result and give value, which brings better leads.
4. **Hooks: 10 (aim for 50) on one body and one CTA.** Most are industry callouts.
5. **Offer:** state the logo and the $49 up front, give a true reason it's free, and say who it's not for.

## 5. How He'd Judge the Test

- **First cut:** at about 20 leads per hook, kill the hooks with the worst 3-second view rate and cost per lead.
- **Verdict:** compare cost per buyer and 30-day cash per ad dollar, not cost per lead. Today it's about $38 per buyer ($5 ÷ 13%). A $7 lead that buys at 20% ($35) beats a $4 lead at 8% ($50).
- **Lead quality:** score each lead by the website they typed: real business, agency, or neither.
- **Weak leads:** add good friction (sharper callouts) before changing the offer.
- **Winner:** make 100 versions and split budget 70/20/10. The goal is 30-day gross profit at or above ad spend.
