**Hook (ChatGPT teaser, first 20 seconds)**

"I just asked ChatGPT who the best roofer in Austin is. It named three companies. Thousands of roofers in Austin, and only three got named. If you're not one of them, a customer who asks this question never even hears about you.
In part 3 I'll show you exactly how to get on that list. But first, there's something that has to be right before any of that matters."

**Intro**
"I'm Szilard. My team and I have been making videos and websites for small businesses since 2008. In this short series I'll show you the 3 things your website needs to actually get you clients.
One: the design, so people understand what you sell. Two: a video, so they trust you and call. Three: traffic, so people find you on Google and ChatGPT.
Today, number one."

**The 6 parts every homepage needs**
"When someone lands on your website, they decide in a few seconds if they stay. So your homepage needs to answer one question fast: what do you sell, and is it for me?
Here's the order that works:
1. A headline that says what you do and for who. Not 'Welcome to our website'. Something like 'Pool builders in Bakersfield, from design to the first swim.'
2. One great image. Your work, your team, a happy customer. Not a stock photo.
3. A few bullet points. What you offer, why you, what makes you different.
4. Your work so far. Pictures of real jobs. Before and after, if you have it.
5. Testimonials. Real customers, real names, real words.
6. Then everything else: your services, your blog, your content."
[Screen: a homepage with each section highlighted as he names it]

**Mobile**
"And one more thing. About 8 out of 10 people will look at your website on their phone. So check it on your phone right now. Can you read the headline without zooming? Can you tap the phone number? If not, you're losing customers before they even read anything."

**Call to action**
"Here's what I can do for you. Add your website below this video, and my team will redesign your homepage on these principles and send you the full picture, free. We'll also check how Google and ChatGPT see your website today.
In part 2, I'll show you the one thing that turns a visitor into a phone call. See you there."

## Part 2: The video (about 5 min)

**Hook**
"Most people who land on your website leave without calling. Not because they don't need you. Because nothing on the page made them trust you enough to pick up the phone. Today I'll show you the fastest fix."

**Why a video**
"A video on your homepage does what text can't. They see you, they hear you, and in one minute they know if you're the right person for the job.
The best one is you on camera. Explain what you do, show a few examples, show your work.
If you don't want to be on camera, that's fine. At least have a 30 second to 1 minute explainer: what you do and why your offer is special."
[Screen: example of a homepage with a video at the top]

**The 3 parts of every video**
"Every good video has just 3 parts.
One, a hook. The first few seconds decide if they keep watching, so start with their problem, not your logo.
Two, the offer. What you do and why you, said clearly, without wasting their time.
Three, the call to action. Tell them exactly what to do next. Call this number. Book a time. Get a quote.
Most business videos miss at least one of these."

**How to make it**
"You can make this video yourself. We built a tool for it, the AI Media Machine. It runs on these same 3 rules. You type in your website, and it writes and makes the video for you in a few minutes.
Or my team can make it for you, from your footage, your photos, or from scratch."

**Call to action**
"Add your website below this video, and we'll send you your free homepage redesign and your Google and ChatGPT check.
In part 3, I'll show you how to get found, on Google and on ChatGPT. That's the part most businesses are missing. See you there."

## Part 3: Getting found on Google and ChatGPT (about 6 min)

**Hook**
"Remember the roofers in Austin? ChatGPT named three. Today I'll show you why it picked them, and what you can do so it picks you."

**Why this matters now**
"A website nobody sees doesn't get you any clients. For years that meant Google. Now more and more people just ask ChatGPT: where can I find a plumber, a roofer, a dentist near me. ChatGPT recommends a few businesses based on where they are and what they need.
So you need to be on Google, and now also in the AI answers."

**Tip 1: let the AI in**
"Many websites, especially WordPress ones, still block robots from reading the site. When ChatGPT reads your website, it is a robot. So a lot of businesses are invisible to it and don't even know.
Your web person can fix this in a few minutes. Or my team can."

**Tip 2: a short summary on every page**
"Every page on your website should have a short description at the top of its code. It's called the meta description. It's the little summary you see under a link on Google.
For years nobody cared about it. Now AI search reads it to understand what the page is about. If it's empty, you're making ChatGPT guess."

**Tip 3: answer the questions your customers ask**
[Owner's choice; he forgot his third tip while recording the idea. This fills it.]
"Put the real questions your customers ask on your pages, with short, clear answers. How much does it cost? How long does it take? Do you serve my area? That's exactly what people type into ChatGPT, and it looks for pages that answer it."

**Tip 4: a short video for each page, on YouTube**
"And here's the one most people miss. Make a short video for each important page and put it on YouTube.
[Screen: his YouTube analytics, traffic sources]
This is my own YouTube channel. Look where the views come from: Google search. People search on Google and find my video directly.
So when someone searches 'roofers near Austin', your website can show up, and your video can show up on the same page. Two chances instead of one."

**Proof**
"We did this for one of our clients, Ascent Equipment. In 14 days, his website went from not showing up in AI answers at all to showing up in half of them, his site loaded more than twice as fast, and his organic traffic went up 45%."
[Screen: the Ascent before/after numbers: grade 71 to 93, load 10.2s to 4.6s, AI answers 0% to 50%]

**The machine**
"Think about how many businesses in your industry have a proper website, a proper video, and pages ready for Google and ChatGPT. Very few. They're busy running the business.
What if your marketing worked like a machine, bringing you interested customers from the two places people search: Google and ChatGPT?"

**Call to action (the full offer)**
"Theory is nice. Practice is better. So here's what I'll do for you.
Add your website below this video. My team will:
One, redesign your homepage on the principles from part 1, and send you the full picture, free.

If you like what you see, we can build the whole site for you, every page ready for Google and AI search from day one, plus the videos. And if not, the mockup and the check are yours to keep.
