# BlueFx YouTube Playbook: 60 Free Video Ad Episodes

Written and checked on 2026-10-15. All 60 search phrases came back as YouTube autocomplete suggestions on Oct 15, and the search results page was checked for the new 30 and for the strongest of the first 30. Every AI Media Machine edit in this playbook was checked against the app's code ("Edit this video" in The Phantom), so each episode shows a feature that really exists.

You record 1 episode every weekday, and 1 goes out every weekday from Mon Oct 19 to Fri Jan 8.

## 1. The plan

One episode goes out every weekday, Monday to Friday, from Mon Oct 19, 2026 to Fri Jan 8, 2027. That's 60 episodes in 12 weeks. You record each one 2 weekdays before it publishes (the first on Thu Oct 15 for Mon Oct 19) and send it to Claude. Claude edits it and sends it back with the title, description, pinned comment, thumbnail and a Short, as section 5 describes. You schedule each upload in YouTube Studio, so it goes out on its date even on a holiday. Section 4 has every date. Section 5 has the daily rhythm and the Christmas and New Year changes.

Every episode teaches a phrase that owners really type into YouTube. On this channel, plain AI ad reveals flopped, and videos that teach a searched phrase get thousands of views. The first 6 episodes (Mon Oct 19 to Mon Oct 26) cover each business type once, plus the phrase that matches the free tool itself (YouTube page 1 as seen on Oct 15):

- **A pizzeria first.** The top 2 videos for "how to make ai video for restaurant" are about a month old, with 4,537 and 505 views. The channel's AI recipe video already shows on page 1 (#11).
- **A candle store.** Page 1 is candle owners' channels talking strategy. None of them shows how to make a video ad, and a 295-view video sits at #6. The people searching are real owners.
- **A real estate agent.** Small channels rank here (a 181-view video is #4), and page 1 is photo walkthroughs. Nobody shows a website being turned into an ad.
- **A car detail shop.** Page 1 is showreels 3 to 6 years old, with no how-to.
- **A roofer.** These are high-ticket owners with ad budgets. Page 1 is agencies talking about running roofing ads plus old commercials. Nobody shows how to make the video, and a 197-view video is #6.
- **"url to video ai free".** People searching this want exactly what the free tool does. Tool companies hold the top spots, but videos with about 2,400 views still make page 1.

After that the types keep rotating, so no two episodes in a row feature the same kind of business. This also shows you quickly which type brings in the most free ad requests.

Every video gets its own ref tag (yt-food-01, yt-ecom-02 and so on) in the description link and the pinned comment. The "Opt-in page conversion" panel in the admin area has a "By tag" view that counts visitors, opt-ins and sales under each tag, and it leaves your own tests out. At 7 days and 28 days after each episode goes out, write down:
- views
- free ad requests
- requests per 1,000 views (requests divided by views, times 1,000)
- any buyer whose request carries that tag

Both checks land on the same weekday as the publish date. So each weekday you log the episode from 1 week ago, and from Mon Nov 16 on you also log the one from 4 weeks ago.

Three dates decide what comes next:
- **Call 1, Mon Nov 9.** With the 7-day numbers for #1 to #10, compare requests per 1,000 views by business type.
- **Call 2, Mon Dec 7.** Make the same comparison with the 28-day numbers for #1 to #10 and the 7-day numbers for #1 to #30.
- **Mon Dec 28.** Plan the next 60 episodes from the results. By then the 28-day numbers for #1 to #30 and the 7-day numbers for #1 to #45 are in.

At each call, a type has earned more slots when its episodes bring at least double the channel's average requests per 1,000 views, or when one of its episodes reaches the top 5 for its phrase. Doubling down means moving that type's spare topics (listed at the end of section 3) into the later slots of the weaker types, starting with any type whose episodes all land under half the channel's average. Keep the rule of no two episodes in a row on the same type, and give each moved episode its own ref tag. Make the swaps from the next weekly site session on (section 5), so no site you've already tested goes to waste.

If you miss a recording day, record 2 the next day. If you can't catch up, keep the same order and move all the later episodes to later dates.

## 2. The episode template

### Set up before you hit record

- Tab 1: the business website. Tab 2: the free tool (https://app.bluefx.net/free-video-ad). Tab 3: the AI Media Machine, logged in.
- The episode's edit note, written word for word on a sticky note (from the topic list).
- The on-screen note ready: **"Demo. Not affiliated with this business"**. Keep it small, in a bottom corner, from the first frame of the website to the end. If you forget it, Claude adds it in the edit.

### Minute by minute (6 to 8 minutes, horizontal, screen recording with your voice)

- [ ] **0:00** Flash the free ad (the free tool's version, before any edit) for 3 to 5 seconds and say the opening line. Say the search phrase out loud here.
- [ ] **0:20** Show the real website. Scroll the homepage, the gallery or menu, and the footer social links. Show there is no video anywhere. Say 2 nice, true things (the photos, the offer, the story, the years in business).
- [ ] **1:00** Paste the website into the free tool. Say "the link is the first one in the description." Claude cuts the wait.
- [ ] **2:30** Play the free result once, start to finish. Read 2 or 3 of the "Why this ad works" bullets under it. Say once that the presenter is a made-up AI person, not the owner or anyone who works there.
- [ ] **3:30** Say one or two things you would change, kindly ("I'd love their own colors in it", "I'd open on their best before and after").
- [ ] **4:00** Go to tab 3: your free ad is already in your Phantom videos as "Your free video ad for [website]". (With your own email address, the free tool shows you the same page a visitor sees. The "Open your video ad in the AI Media Machine" link is only at the end of the FAQ answer "What happens to my free video ad?", so it is quicker to switch to tab 3.) Open it, go to "Edit this video" and make the episode's change from the topic list. If you see two copies for the same website (your off-camera test and today's run), open the newest one, and if the edit note names a scene number, check that the scene still shows what the note expects. Type the note on screen so viewers can copy it. While it renders, say why this change matters for this kind of business.
- [ ] **5:30** Side by side: first the plain website next to the finished ad, then the free version next to the edited one.
- [ ] **6:00** CTA (lines below). End screen: the latest live episode of the same business type (for #1 to #6, one of your other videos), since an end screen can only point to a video that is already public.

### The opening line pattern

"This is a real **[business type]** with no video anywhere on its website, and I made this **[search phrase or 'video ad']** for it for free in 3 minutes, so let me show you exactly how."

Examples:
- "This is a real pizza shop with no video anywhere on its website, and I made this AI video ad for it for free in 3 minutes, so let me show you exactly how."
- "If you've wondered how to make real estate ads without hiring an editor, watch this: a real agent's website with zero video, turned into this ad for free in three minutes."

### The CTA lines (use the same ones every episode)

- "Comment your website below, and I may feature it in a future video."
- "Or make yours free right now with the first link in the description. Paste your website and you get your video ad."
- "Everything I changed at minute 4 happened in the AI Media Machine. That's the second link."

Never say the ad will bring sales, calls, leads or orders.

### Rules for every episode

- Whenever the business is on screen, show the note "Demo. Not affiliated with this business".
- Say only nice things about the business. Never imply it is a customer, or that it made or uses the ad.
- Skip businesses whose photos are mostly people's faces (staff headshots, diners, customers, models).
- Never use professional real estate listing photos. Use agent sites and community pages, and skip any site whose free result pulls listing photos.
- The presenter is always a made-up AI person. It is never the owner, a staff member or any real or named person.
- Use only facts that are on the website (offers, dates, prices, phone numbers).
- No promises about sales, calls, leads, orders or results.
- Blur license plates and anything private.

### The 4:00 edit: what the AI Media Machine can really do

Good to know first:
- Your own free video ads are copied into your AI Media Machine (your Phantom videos) without the watermark. If a copy still shows the mark, the first edit makes a clean version.
- Every edit makes a new version and keeps the old one, so you can play both. An edit costs 10 credits. Ticking "Change the presenter" adds 30 credits, and you get those back if the presenter cannot be made.
- Only what the note asks for changes. If the note changes no spoken line, the recorded voice-over is kept. If it changes any narrated line (a new first line, new words, a new voice), the whole voice-over is recorded again.

| Edit | How to do it | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| New first line (hook) | Type the new line in "Edit this video", tick **"Change the presenter"** and write "the same person". | The AI presenter says the first line, so a note alone leaves it as it was. 16 words at most. |
| New presenter | Tick **"Change the presenter"** and write who should be on camera ("a man in his 60s in a work polo"). | Always a made-up person. Never the business owner, a real person or a named person. Vertical videos only. |
| Voice | Note: "Use a warmer, calmer voice for the voice-over." | Changes the narrator of the other scenes. The presenter keeps their own voice. |
| Music | Note: "Use slower, warmer acoustic music." | A new track is made. Play the same 5 seconds with the old and the new music. |
| Signature sound | Note: "Add the hiss of an espresso machine after the first scene." | One short real-world sound. |
| New photo or clip | Button **"Add a new photo, logo or clip"**, then a note: "Use this new photo right after the presenter." | If you don't say where, it replaces the weakest or most repeated picture. In the whiteboard look it is taped to the board. Nothing goes over the presenter. |
| Brand | Upload their logo, note: "Use this logo and match the colors to it." | The new logo replaces the old one everywhere and the colors follow it. |
| Words or end card | Note: "Change the last scene to: ..." | Facts only from the website. A changed line means the whole voice-over is recorded again. |
| Captions | Note: "Add captions to every scene." | The word "captions" (or "subtitles") must be in the note. Without it, the free ad keeps its subtitles under the presenter only. |
| Voice-over off | Switch **Voice-over** off on the edit panel and type "Show captions on every scene." | Same reason: without "captions" in the note, the narrated scenes play with no words. |
| Length | Note: "Make it shorter, about 15 seconds." | Keeps the presenter's opening. |
| Look | Note: "Use the Bold look." (Playful, Elegant, Bold, Clean, Whiteboard) | Try it once off camera before recording. |
| Drawing | Note: "Redraw the drawing in scene 2 as ..." | Whiteboard look only. No words or numbers inside a drawing. |
| Horizontal version | A new video in The Phantom: paste the same website and set Format to **Horizontal**. | This is a new video, not an edit. The AI presenter is only in vertical videos. |
| Video Maker clip | In Video Maker, use a photo from the site as First Frame (add a Last Frame for a before and after). Then add the clip to the ad with "Add a new photo, logo or clip". | The only way to get motion into the ad. |
| Find Video Ads | Look up top ads for the business type, then use their opening style for a new first line. | Counts as the new first line edit in the rotation. |

An edit cannot add moving photos ("Photos that move"), a UGC creator remake, motion graphics, a zoom into a detail or text that changes color. If you ask for one, it does the closest thing it can and tells you what it couldn't do. For motion, use a Video Maker clip.

## 5. The recording kit

### Find a business with no video in 5 minutes

1. **Minute 0 to 1.** Google "[business type] [city]". For roofers use a city in a hail or storm state. For agents use a beach, mountain or small town. Skip ads, directories, chains and franchises. Open 5 business websites in tabs.
2. **Minute 1 to 3.** Spend about 30 seconds on each tab:
   - Scroll the homepage and look for a video player or a YouTube or Vimeo embed.
   - Open the gallery or menu page.
   - Click the footer social icons and check their YouTube and Facebook for videos and Reels.
   - For restaurants and trades, also search the name in the Meta Ad Library for video ads.
3. **Minute 3 to 4.** Photo test: mostly products, food, places or finished work. Few faces. No professional listing photos. Skip any site where you'd have to blur half the pictures.
4. **Minute 4 to 5.** Praise test: find 2 true nice things (an offer line, a story, years in business, a specialty). Address test: it must be their own website. The free tool refuses Amazon, Etsy, eBay, Zillow, Realtor.com, Facebook, Instagram, Google, Yelp, Linktree and similar pages.

Keep the 2 or 3 sites that pass.

### Pre-recording checklist

- [ ] Run the 2 or 3 sites through the free tool with your own BlueFx email address, using https://app.bluefx.net/free-video-ad?ref=owner-test. The funnel panel leaves your address and the owner-test tag out of its numbers, and your address has no limit on free ads.
- [ ] Pick the best result and download it. Check that:
  - the presenter's line sounds right
  - the photos are from the site
  - there are no faces you'd rather not show and no listing photos
  - the offer made it in
  - it makes no health, safety or money claims and promises no results
  - the "Why this ad works" bullets are good
- [ ] Open its copy in your AI Media Machine and try the planned edit once off camera. Always do this for a Look change, a horizontal version and a new first line.
- [ ] Write down the search phrase, the business name, the website, 2 nice things, 1 or 2 things to change, and the edit note word for word.
- [ ] On camera, paste the same website again with your own address. This replaces your earlier test of that website in the free tool, which is why you download the test first. If the new result comes out weaker, show the downloaded test instead (same tool, same website).
- [ ] Screen setup: one browser window with 3 tabs, bookmarks bar hidden, notifications off, page zoomed in enough to read on a phone. Do a 10-second mic check.
