**Using the directories.** Most pages state no terms. But IAEE, the organizers' trade group, says outside use of exhibitor lists "is strictly prohibited without permission," and Informa (WOC, IRE, WWETT, MD&M West) bans "solicitation purposes of any kind" on another show's list. So take only names and websites, find contacts on each company's own site, use no show logos, and follow CAN-SPAM.

## Part 2: Emails (Instantly)

Send from separate warmed-up domains, never support@bluefx.net.

**Email 1, Day 0 (3 to 5 weeks before the show)**

Subject: {company}, your booth video for {show}

> Hi {first_name},
>
> I made a 40-second video ad for {company} from your website, with a presenter, voice-over and music. It can play on your booth screen at {show}.
>
> Watch it here: {link}
>
> Want one change, like your booth number? Reply and I will make it for free.
>
> Why free: some companies pay $49 for the clean version, and a few hire us to build their website.
>
> The free version has a small BlueFx logo. The $49 version has no logo and is made for a booth screen: a 16:9 loop with captions, so it works with no sound.
>
> Szilard
> BlueFx, video ads for businesses since 2008
>
> Not affiliated with {show}. [Postal address]. Reply "no" and I will not email again.

**Email 2, Day 3**

Subject: Re: {company}, your booth video for {show}

> Hi {first_name},
>
> Did you see the video I made for {company}? Here it is again: {link}
>
> If you want the $49 booth version, order it a week before {show} so you can test it on your screen.
>
> Szilard

**Email 3, Day 8**

Subject: Should I delete it?

> Hi {first_name},
>
> I have not heard back, so I guess {company} does not need the booth video. I will delete it in 7 days.
>
> To keep it: {link}. The free version is yours. The clean booth version is $49.
>
> Good luck at {show}.
>
> Szilard
> BlueFx, video ads for businesses since 2008

Then expire the link.

## Part 3: Numbers and timing

**Funnel, top 6 shows (about 5,350 exhibitors)**

| Step | Rate | Count |
|---|---|---|
| Decision-maker email found | 60% | about 3,210 |
| Reply or click | 3 to 8% | about 96 to 257 |
| $49 clean version (upper bound) | 6% of engaged | up to 6 to 15 (about $290 to $750) |
| Website client ($299 to $1,499/mo) | 1 per 100 to 200 engaged | about 0.5 to 2.6 |

All 15 shows (about 15,650 exhibitors, some overlap): 282 to 751 engaged, up to 17 to 45 sales at $49, 1.4 to 7.5 website clients.

At about $1 per video (holiday-ads-plan.md), 3,210 videos cost about $3,200, more than the $49 sales. Website clients must carry it: one at $1,499/mo repays it in about 2 months, at $299/mo in about 11. Drop big brands and non-US firms first.
