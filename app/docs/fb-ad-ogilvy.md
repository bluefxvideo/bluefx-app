# BlueFx Free Video Ad: Facebook/Instagram Script

Written with Ogilvy's principles and the HJ10 method.

## 1. Ogilvy's diagnosis

1. No news. Neither draft says what is new: AI now writes the script from your own words and photos and emails the ad.
2. Generalities, not facts. "Want more customers" fits any agency. The facts (40 seconds, nothing filmed, 220,000+ leads, since 2008) are missing.
3. The hooks promise nothing specific, and the brand and demonstration come late.
4. Research: the longest-running Ad Library ads (Adobe's free Premiere launch, 208 days; Trainwell, 106 days) open with news or a hard number.

## 2. Version 1: "New" (news + demonstration)

HJ10: Experimenter hook (show the tool working), with visual, text and spoken hook aligned. CTA at the end only.

| 0-3s HOOK | "New: BlueFx AI makes your video ad in 5 minutes." | Web address typed into BlueFx, progress bar, finished ad plays. | NEW: TYPE YOUR WEBSITE. GET A VIDEO AD IN 5 MINUTES. |
| 3-11s | "Type your website. AI writes a script from your words and photos, adds a presenter, voice and music, then emails it." | Presenter on top. Below: site photos dropping into a timeline, then the email. | YOUR WORDS + YOUR PHOTOS = 40-SECOND AD |

## 3. Pick: Version 1

1. **The headline carries news, the brand and a specific benefit**, all in 3 seconds. Draft A opened with a question any agency could ask, and draft B with the mechanism and no brand.
2. **Show the product working.** Version 1 shows the website going in and the ad coming out, then proves it with a real ad: "Nobody filmed anything." The rejected drafts only said it could be done.

Test Version 2 against it: 220,000 leads is BlueFx's strongest fact.
