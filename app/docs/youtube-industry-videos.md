# YouTube series: a free video ad for every industry

Owner idea, 2026-11-06 ("we can definitely do one for each"). This is one video per industry on @bluefxvideo. Each video makes a real free video ad for a real business in that industry, live. Then it shows how to run that video ad on Facebook for $5 a day. About 60% of the channel's views come from YouTube search, so every title starts with the words people in that trade actually type into YouTube.

Each video links to app.bluefx.net/free-video-ad with its own tag: `?ref=yt-{slug}` in the description and `?ref=ytc-{slug}` in the pinned comment. The funnel already counts yt- and ytc- tags as YouTube, so the admin funnel panel shows the leads and sales from each video.

## The short version
- **There are 50 videos, ranked below. Make the 15 numbered in the "Make first" column first.** At two a week, the first 15 are out in about 8 weeks.
- **Titles start with the search, not the tool.** Owners don't search "video ad for HVAC". They search "HVAC marketing". So the title is "HVAC Marketing: Free Video Ad Made by AI in 5 Minutes", and the video ad is the answer to that search. The recent free-tool videos (95 to 300 views) did the opposite: they started with the tool.
- **Every industry uses the same 7-minute template** (below): the hook, the video ad made live, the finished video ad, Facebook for $5 a day, one tip for that trade, and the link.
- **Don't say the price on camera.** The unlock costs $49 for 3 days from a lead's first visit to the unlock page, then $99, and a YouTube video stays up for years. "Pay once to get the video ad clean" will always be true.

## What the research found
vidIQ, 2026-11-06: 25 calls, 125 credits (1,714 down to 1,589). The volumes are vidIQ's estimates of monthly YouTube searches worldwide. Competition is vidIQ's score from 0 to 100, where lower is easier. Use these numbers to compare industries, not as exact counts.

1. **Almost nobody searches "video ad for {industry}".** I checked 104 phrases that contain "video ad" or "video ads for". Real estate was the only trade with real volume ("real estate ad video", 9,200 a month, competition 18). The rest were about AI tools and product ads: "ai video ad generator" 17,100, "saas ad video" 13,300, "product ad video" 12,600.
2. **What owners do search, by pattern:**
   - "{industry} marketing" is the strongest pattern overall: HVAC, salons, restaurants, dentists, med spas, law firms, landscaping, contractors, real estate.
   - "facebook ads for {industry}": landscapers, coaches, ecommerce, contractors, financial advisors, bakeries, car dealerships, appliance repair, clinics.
   - "marketing for {industry}": contractors, accounting firms, insurance agents, property management.
   - "{industry} marketing ideas": real estate, gyms, dental clinics, auto mechanics, vets.
   - "{industry} advertising" never appeared in vidIQ's related keywords for any trade, so it's weaker than "marketing" on YouTube. I didn't test it on its own, to save credits.
3. **For many trades, "{trade} marketing" has no measurable searches** (vidIQ shows under 750 a month). This held for roofing, chiropractic, cleaning business, coffee shop, photography business, loan officer, electrician, solar, yoga studio, veterinary clinic, moving company and manufacturing. For these trades the table uses a nearby phrase that does get searches, such as "roofing leads", "cafe marketing", "mortgage leads" or "solar ads".
4. **Competition is low.** Most target phrases score between 6 and 25. Today's results for "hvac video ad" are two production-company reels (1,100 and 4,300 views), a 41-minute marketing podcast, one Facebook ads tutorial (3,000 views) and HVAC repair videos. None of them shows an owner making a video ad. That's the gap this series fills.
5. **Check which countries the searches come from.** Half of the "real estate marketing" searches come from Bangladesh (the US is 21%). Clothing brand content also looks heavy in South Asia: the top "facebook ads for" phrase is "how to run facebook ads for your clothing brand sinhala" (43,900). The funnel blocks those countries, so those views can't become leads.
6. **Later: Spanish.** The same searches in Spanish are just as big and just as open: "marketing inmobiliario" 20,700 (competition 31), "marketing para dentistas" 17,300 (17), "marketing automotriz" 17,100 (20). A Spanish run is worth doing once the free video ad can be made in Spanish.

## How the ranking works
Score = monthly searches x (100 minus competition) / 100. In other words, the searches are discounted by how crowded the phrase is. For each industry, the target keyword is its best phrase from the patterns above.

Two changes for the first 15:
- **Clothing brands waits**, even though they score #1, because much of that search comes from countries the funnel blocks. Test them after the first 15.
- **Online stores (#16) moves in**, because a product video ad is the channel's biggest winner ("AI Recipe Videos with Your Product", 48x the usual views).

## The video template (about 7 minutes)
The same script works for every industry, whether Szilard hosts or his AI presenter does. Fill in the {fields}, and take the industry lines from the fill-in table below. Keep the screen recordings real (the form, the status page, the email), because the live part is the proof.

### Before you film
1. **Pick a real business in that trade** whose website has real photos, a clear list of services, a city and a phone number. A past client or free video ad lead in that trade is best. If you don't have one, ask a local business first: "Can I make a free video ad from your website on my YouTube channel? You'll get the finished video ad free, without the stamp." Don't feature a business without asking.
2. **Fill in the form with contact@bluefx.net.** That address has no caps, and the lead stays out of the funnel numbers. Two side effects:
   - Once you've made a video ad for a site, the tool tells that business "{site} already has its free video ad" if they try the site later. Send them their video ad yourself.
   - If the business already got a free video ad with its own email, the tool refuses the site. Pick another business.
3. **Open app.bluefx.net/free-video-ad?internal=1 once in the browser you film with.** That marks the browser as internal for a year, so your own visits don't count in that video's yt- numbers.
4. **Write down the real numbers:** how many minutes the video ad took, and the first line the AI wrote.
5. **Don't use a Facebook page or a marketplace listing as the website.** The tool refuses social and marketplace pages.

### Script
**0:00 to 0:15. Hook**
- On screen: 4 seconds of the finished video ad, with sound. Then cut to the host, with the video ad playing on a phone. Show "{target keyword}" as on-screen text. Saying and showing the keyword helps YouTube match the video to the search.
- Say: "This video ad is for {Business}, a {industry} company in {City}. I typed their website into a free AI tool, and about {X} minutes later the video ad was ready, with a presenter, a voice-over and music. I'll make one live right now, then show you how to run the video ad on Facebook for $5 a day."

- Say: "If you're looking for {target keyword} that's simple and cheap, start here. Most {customers} pick the {industry} company they've already seen. A 40-second video ad on Facebook and Instagram puts your name, your work and your phone number in front of people near you for a few dollars a day. The link to make yours is in the description. You get one free video ad per business, and you don't need a credit card."

**0:45 to 1:15. The website**
- On screen: the business's homepage, scrolling slowly.
- Say: "Here's {Business}'s website. The AI reads two things. It reads the words, so it knows the services, the city and the phone number. And it reads the photos, which go into the scenes. A site with photos of real jobs makes a better video ad."

**1:15 to 3:30. Make the video ad, live**
- On screen: app.bluefx.net/free-video-ad?ref=yt-{slug}
- Say: "I go to app.bluefx.net/free-video-ad. The link is in the description. I type the website, {website}, and click the button. Then it asks for a first name and an email, because the video ad comes by email."
- On the status page, say: "Now the AI Media Machine does the work. It writes a script from the website, picks an AI presenter, records the voice-over, adds music and draws the scenes."
- While the video ad is being made, say the 3 rules for a {industry} video ad:
  1. "One: say what you do and where, in the first 3 seconds. 'Need {service} in {City}?'"
  2. "Two: one offer. For example, {offer}."
  3. "Three: one action. Call, book or ask for a quote. Pick one, not all three."
- Jump cut to the "your video ad is ready" email. Say: "That took {X} minutes today." Say the real time, because on a busy day there's a queue.

**3:30 to 4:30. The finished video ad**
- On screen: the whole video ad, full screen, with sound (about 40 seconds).
- Say: "Here's why this works for a {industry} company. It opens with {the first line}, it shows {their photos}, and it ends with {their call to action}. If you want something changed, like adding your offer, you get one change free on your video ad page. The free version has a small SAMPLE stamp and a short BlueFX end card. **4:30 to 6:30. Run the video ad on Facebook for $5 a day**
- On screen: Meta Ads Manager, one step at a time.
- Say:
  1. "Download the video ad from your video ad page."
  2. "In Facebook Ads Manager, click Create, choose Engagement, and set the goal to video views."
  3. "Set the budget to $5 a day, and run the ad for 7 days before you judge it."
  4. "For location, pick your town plus about 15 miles. For age, pick 25 and up. Leave the rest on Advantage+."
  5. "The video ad is vertical, so it fits Reels and Stories. Leave placements on Advantage+ too."
  6. "Write one line of text: '{Service} in {City}. {Offer}. Call {phone}.' Then pick the Learn more or Call now button."
  7. "After 7 days, make an audience of people who watched at least half of the video ad. Show them a second ad with your offer, also $5 a day. These people already know you."
- Only for real estate, mortgage, property management, insurance and financial advisors, say: "Facebook puts these ads in a special ad category: Housing, or Financial products and services. You can't narrow the age, and the radius starts at 15 miles. That's fine for a test."
- The easy way, say: "If Ads Manager feels like too much, post the video ad on your Facebook page and tap Boost. Pick $5 a day for 7 days, and people near you."
- Then say: "Before you put money behind the video ad, get the clean version, so people see your name and not ours."

**6:30 to 7:00. One tip for {industry}**
- Say the tip from the fill-in table.

**7:00 to 7:30. Get yours**

### Thumbnail formula
- **Left side (about 55%):** a phone showing the best frame of the finished video ad, with the presenter's face visible mid-sentence.
- **Right side:** three big words: **FREE {INDUSTRY} AD**, for example FREE HVAC AD. The industry word is the biggest, because it's the word the searcher is looking for.
- **One small tag in a corner:** "AI, 5 MIN".
- **Background:** a photo from the trade (an AC unit, a kitchen, a lawn), blurred so the text stands out.
- **Optional:** Szilard pointing at the phone, like the photo on the landing page. If you use it, use it in every video.
- **Same layout every time.** Only the industry word, the frame and the background change, so the playlist looks like one series. Check the thumbnail at phone size: if you can't read the industry word at 160 pixels wide, make it bigger.
- **No business logos** unless the business said yes.

## How to tell what's working
- **After 28 days, check each video in two places.** In YouTube Studio, check how many views came from YouTube search, and which search terms brought them. In the admin funnel panel, check the leads and sales for yt-{slug} and ytc-{slug}.
- **If an industry brings sales, give it a second video.** For example, an HVAC tune-up ad before summer, or a dentist ad for one service.
- **If a video gets search impressions but few clicks after 2 weeks,** change the thumbnail first, then the title.
- **Once the first 15 are out, test clothing brands.** Then decide on a Spanish run.
